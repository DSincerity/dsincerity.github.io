2025

  1. EMNLP
    ASTRA: A Negotiation Agent with Adaptive and Strategic Reasoning via Tool-integrated Action for Dynamic Offer Optimization
    Deuksin Kwon, J. Hae, E. Clift, D. Shamsoddini, Jonathan Gratch, and Gale Lucas
    In Proceedings of the 2025 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing, 2025
    TL;DR Link

    A stateful, tool-augmented LLM agent with online belief-state updates (partner preferences, fairness, stance), solver-backed tool use, and look-ahead simulation for strategic action selection. Improves agent utility from 20.75 to 24.31 (+17.2%) in agreement cases.
