**Context.** AI Research Intern, Honda Research Institute, San Jose (May–Aug 2026).

- Built **closed-loop multi-agent human behavior simulations** and **human-referenced process-fidelity evaluation**, tracking cross-speaker uptake and group solution progress — getting the *right answer* isn't enough if the group gets there differently than humans do.
- Developed **human-aligned policy calibration and LLM post-training** from participant decision boundaries, learning *when* simulated agents wait, speak, and respond.

📄 _Beyond Right Answers: Diagnosing and Calibrating Human-Referenced Process Fidelity in Multi-Agent LLM Deliberation_ — first author, submitted to NAACL 2027.
