- **Pretrained foundation LLMs from scratch.**
- Built **RAG-based long-term-memory conversational agents** for the publicly launched AI assistant _A._ ([ACL 2023 Industry Track](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-industry.68/)).
- Built a **customer-service conversational AI system** (launched 2020) and **internal ML modeling platforms** (deployed 2019).
- Co-authored Korean/English LM benchmarks: [KoBEST](https://aclanthology.org/2022.coling-1.325/) and [BECEL](https://aclanthology.org/2022.coling-1.324/) (COLING 2022).
