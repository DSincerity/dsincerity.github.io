**Question.** When LLM agents simulate people, do they actually *behave* like people — or just produce plausible text?

**Work.**

- Built **persona-grounded conversational agents** deployed in a real product (SK Telecom's AI assistant _A._, [ACL 2023 Industry](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-industry.68/)).
- Built **personality-conditioned multi-agent simulations** matched to real human dispute-resolution scenarios ([AAAI 2026](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v40i45.41223)).
- Designed **human-referenced behavioral evaluation** across linguistic coordination, emotion dynamics, strategic behavior, and personality–behavior alignment ([EMNLP 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.828/)).

**Finding.** Systematic gaps between simulated and human behavior that outcome-only metrics miss.
