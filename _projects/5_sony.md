**Collaboration** with Sony Interactive Entertainment (PlayStation), ongoing since 2025.

Developing LLM-based persuasive agents for games that adapt their persuasion strategy using **Construal Level Theory** — shifting between abstract (why) and concrete (how) framing depending on the player.

📄 _Learning to Persuade in Games: A Construal Level Theory–Driven Persuasive Agent_ — manuscript in preparation.
